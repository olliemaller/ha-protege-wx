# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Home Assistant or alarm systems;
- respecting different viewpoints and experience;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- owning our mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what's best for the people who use this.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults or personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information, such as addresses, alarm codes or details of their security system, without their explicit permission;
- anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities

The maintainer will clarify these standards and take fair action on any behaviour they consider inappropriate. That can mean removing or editing comments, code, issues and other contributions, and temporarily or permanently banning someone from the project.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and the wiki) and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@olliemaller](https://github.com/olliemaller), through GitHub. You can also use **Report content** on any comment or issue. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning explaining what was wrong.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no contact with the people involved for a while.
3. **Temporary ban:** no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards anyone.
