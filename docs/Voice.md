# Voice (Assist)

Ask Assist, in English. It works with Home Assistant's built-in conversation agent (an AI agent needs **Prefer handling commands locally** turned on), and answers for every controller you've added.

| Ask | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Is the house armed?" | "The Alarm is armed home." |
| "Can I arm?" | "Not yet: Living and Kitchen are open, but you can arm it in home mode." |
| "Who came in last?" | "Jane, at 6:42 pm at the Front Door." |

Other ways to ask the same thing: "what's the alarm doing", "alarm status", "is it safe to arm", "anything open", "who got home last".

Arming and disarming by voice use Home Assistant's own alarm sentences, and need the alarm panel exposed to Assist.
